RULES FOR EVERY MODEL
- Do not recreate a model that already exists. Skip it.
- Modular pieces on a 1 meter grid: wall panels are exactly 1 m wide, 3 m tall and at most 0.2 m thick, with a flat back, so they can be placed side by side on top of existing walls.
- Maximum 2000 faces per piece. Triangle topology.
- Realistic style, PBR textures, 1024 px textures, worn, dirty and weathered, horror atmosphere.
- One piece per model, centered, no ground.
- No readable text, letters, words or numbers anywhere: use scratches, tally marks, stains, handprints and pictures only.
- Export .glb with the given file name.

NEGATIVE: text, letters, words, numbers, graffiti lettering, logo, watermark, base, pedestal, ground plane, multiple objects, cartoon, toy, low quality
